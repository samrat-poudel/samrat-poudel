Send Me a Message

For direct inquiries, please use the contact methods above. You can also send me an email with:

  • Subject line: Clearly mention the purpose (Research Collaboration, Thesis Supervision, etc.)
  • Your background: Brief introduction about yourself and your organization
  • Project details: Description of your inquiry or project proposal
  • Expected timeline: When would you like to connect or start collaboration

Response time: I aim to respond to all inquiries within 2-3 working days. During academic breaks or conference periods, responses may take longer.
